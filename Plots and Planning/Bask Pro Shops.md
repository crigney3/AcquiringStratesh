Bask is an ancient nation fallen to ruin (which just happens to be Egypt themed to match my new minis.)
Players will discover a large ancient ruin has appeared somewhere (maybe replace the thing in the pits with this, maybe just have it appear outside of town but sealed up somehow.)
It will already be partially unsealed, but players will need to go into it to fully unseal it.
Inside are the ruins of the civilization, including many entombed warriors, spirits, guardians, and severely weakened gods. Without worshippers, the gods have fallen to become little more than sprites, but they remain prideful and mostly quite angry. They will try and get the players to worship them (which may work) but if that isn’t going well, they may try and use traps or undead warriors to kill the players.
However, not every god will have the same goal. 3 are trying a different strategy, as those have connections to the morals and goals of the players, and will use the players arrival and eventual exit to follow them out.
Alyxia already has a god, and gods inside here will refer to Alyxia as “spoken for.”
Alton will be the “main character” of this quest, as he will begin having visions of golden eyes watching him before entering the ruins. His god is the god of written law (notably not justice.)
Adelynn will be followed out by two gods, who she must eventually choose between - one of industry and one of nature.
Nardo could be picked by one of the gods in the dungeon, otherwise he will likely be followed out by a trickster god who will teach that the best mischief requires focus and planning
At some point, open a session with Alton dreaming of golden eyes staring at him from reflective surfaces, and then a giant crocodile eating him
One of the few passive NPCs is Tela’Maron, god of passage, who will let them into the ruins and potentially guide them. He is fully neutral to every occurrence, and prefers to spectate events.
If the ruin appears in the pits, they’ll likely be contracted by their underground contact, and if it appears near the city, they’ll be contracted by the mayor.
