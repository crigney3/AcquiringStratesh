Based on the public homebrew statblock/flavor of the "sleepless".

Important plot points:
1. People have been dying from an unknown disease/magical effect, where they stay up until they die of 7th level exhaustion. There are no other symptoms, and no visible changes outside of the physical effects of exhaustion.
2. Whenever the quest starts, the players will learn that Larry Solidflower has been unable to sleep recently. He will die at the end of the exhaustion period if the players can't stop the sleepless.
3. Halfway through the quest, Adelynn will be affected as well.
	1. If she is, she may notice that ghosts are very nervous around her, as they can see her spirit being leached into the Ethereal plane.
4. The Proprietor is a former Sleepless who realized that if he kept a physical form, and pumped it full of stimulants, he'd sate most of the curse. He will try to hide this from anyone who asks, because he might be targeted as the one causing the deaths, but if the party can convince him, he may be able to help in their eventual fight against the Sleepless (especially since Sleepless exist in the Ethereal plane by default.)
