An enlightened orangutan contracts them to help free his dad from a rich person’s zoo. His dad is not enlightened, leading to… communication difficulties.
Cage is in a large central viewing area of a large walled garden/courtyard
If the party doesn’t succeed on stealthing in/distracting or whatever, they’ll have to contend with an animated shovel, pruning shears, and rope.
[[Ook]] will make it clear that the other orangutan should be pacified or otherwise convinced to help, but is unwilling to come along (players may be able to convince him)
