## Planned Stuff
The session reopens from the moment Adelynn and Nardo disappeared with Alyxia and Alton standing in the main haunted house. They can have a moment to panic and get back in the mood, and then half-giant ghost friend will show up again, letting them know that 1. they're the only people left in the house and 2. There's some sort of weird portal in a nearby room. He has stepped through it and it doesn't seem dangerous, and he thinks it leads to their friends.
Assuming they go through it, narrative will shift to Nardo, who is trapped in total darkness. If he talks, he'll get a response from Urag of the Formidable Five, who will unfortunately have no info about where they are or how to get out. Narrative will then shift to Adelynn, who has the same scenario but with Ashryn instead. Ashryn gives her an important clue - whatever this creature is, it has trouble snuffing out refracted light. Hopefully Adelynn takes this a cue to use her jewelers goggles to refract light that Ashryn creates, and they'll be able to see their surroundings:

A mirror-like floor, as though standing on the surface of a perfectly calm lake
Total darkness in all directions, broken only slightly by large, mostly transparent walls which divide the area into 5 separate "cells".
In the center is a cylindrical room, also mostly transparent, with Brad and the piece of Sa, The Primordial Shade who works with Brad.

Shortly after revealing their surroundings and figuring out what's going on, likely with a bit of monologuing from Brad, Alyxia and Alton will arrive, essentially appearing out of thin air. Brad will be surprised but unperturbed by this, since he's smoking the souls of his dead coworkers to calm down, and also because he gets to have some fun. After a further monologue, Brad will dismiss the glassy walls and send shadowy tentacles after all the characters. The Formidable Five will all fail their save - Adelynn and Nardo can roll the save, but it's a DC 20 wisdom save.

Anyone who fails is possessed temporarily, and combat begins! The odds are heavily against Alton and Alyxia, but there's a trick - dealing damage or affecting the mind of a possessed character will let them re-roll the save with a much lower DC of 14. Alton and Alyxia also have the goatherd ghost, who will let them know that he can also possess one character - it won't break them out of being possessed by Sa, but the conflicting possession commands will result in total incapacitation outside of rolling saving throws.

After dealing with the possessed characters, the fight moves to Brad, who ends up being much easier given that 9 people and a ghost are now attacking him. Once Brad is dead, Sa's connection to the world will fade, and with it the curse on Sennec's House of Spooks and Scares. The reality everyone is in will also fade and deposit them all in the backyard of the haunted house. They'll say their goodbyes to the Formidable Five with whatever their relationship is at that point, collect money from Sennec who will likely ask them to keep some details from the authorities so he isn't shut down (or worse, has to bribe them), and head back.
