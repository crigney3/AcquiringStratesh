-   The garden itself will be mostly peaceful (and likely require stealth to avoid security.) The lock on the cage in the middle can be broken with enough force, but this will awaken the Garden Guardians - an animated hose, an animated broom, and a pair of animated shears. Failures on stealth checks may do the same.
    

-   The hose will try and tie up, incapacitate, or strangle on its turn. Cutting it in half will make more hoses, but each hose will be a lot weaker with easier DCs. Once a quarter of the hose is damaged in any way, it dies.
    

-   DC 15 for grabbing someone’s weapon/restricting movement, DC 14 for strangling (1d6 damage at start of turn), DC 13 for incapacitated. -1 to these for each time the hose has been cut.
    

-   The broom tries for bludgeoning damage, and has the highest movement speed. +3 to hit, 1d6+1 and target is pushed 5 feet away.
    
-   The shears are the slowest, tankiest, and most damaging. To stop this one, players can’t just hurt the handle; they must either break/harm the metal, or trap it so it can’t move.
    

-   Only 15ft movement speed, metal has resistance to most damage types, +4 to hit, 2d6 damage.
    

-   May rebalance these depending on how many people are fighting them.
