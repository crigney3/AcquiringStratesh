# Planned Stuff
Currently under attack by Sleepless: Adelynn, Makenna

The next day begins in gloom as a light rain covers the town and the sun is hidden. The party must continue investigating, and see if maybe they can cure Adelynn without infecting others. Their investigations should be guided towards the Proprietor, or possibly more plotline with the orphans.
If the proprietor can be convinced to trust them and help, he'll clue them in on what a sleepless is, and his method for surviving. Maybe he'll straight up tell them about his formula, who knows. The next challenge the players have is how to get into the Ethereal plane to fight the Sleepless, or possibly, how to lure them out of the Ethereal Plane. Both will be difficult, and will likely requrie paying a powerful spellcaster in the city to help.

The sleepless fight:
If it occurs in the Ethereal Plane, the Sleepless will suffer no penalties but the party will get their gods as familiars who can give the help action (Alyxia will be able to make a check to see if she can commune with Selune, and if she can, she'll be able to use a powerful moonbeam attack)
If it occurs in the physical world, the Sleepless will have disadvantage on saving throws.
Stats:
Improv health
INT +1
WIS -1
STR +2
CON + 4
DEX +1
CHA -1
Immune to sleep and resistant to charmed
Abilities:
Claws: can attack twice per turn, +3 to hit, 2d4 damage, WIS save or gain a stack of exhaustion
Grapple: can grab players, and on a success, begins to drain their soul rapidly. If a player ends their turn still grappled, they take damage equal to half their max HP. The sleepless cannot act if it's grappling a player. When the PC is grappled, they gain one stack of exhaustion.
Wave of Drowsiness: 10ft radius sphere around the sleepless. Causes no damage, but adds a stack of exhaustion.
Players can remove exhaustion through tropey methods of staying awake, like pinching themselves and drinking coffee, or creative ones. I, as well as rolls, determine how many stacks of exhaustion it removes
