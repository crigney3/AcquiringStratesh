# Planned Stuff
Open the session with the party arriving in Tohmond through a teleportation circle. They'll leave the building to see the sights of the capital - thronging crowds and "tall" buildings (about 4 or 5 stories high.) Hahim will instruct them before they go that they will have rooms at an inn downtown that's owned by Acquisitions: The Adventure-In, where Adventurers go in!
They might meet some other adventurers staying here, as well as improv NPCs met around the city. Once that's all happened, the main event will begin with the winter solstice company party, masquerade themed this year. Here's what the players chose to enter with:

Remember to tell the players to describe themselves with some prompting, and dependant on rolls for their entrance too.

Once in the party, they'll have time to mingle with others - make a table of "hero" names below.
Adelynn will encounter at least one of the bounty hunters - a man dressed in formal wear with a simple bugbear mask. He has a russian accent.
After mingling a bit, Alton will be given a letter saying he's invited to the VIP zone to meet someone important. He'll be masked as well, in a very dignified hawklike outfit. This is Jim Darkmagic, and he'll talk to Alton about order and chaos, and how he sees potential in Alton as someone who respects order. If Alton seems agreeable, he'll clue Alton into how the high-ranking research Obviators and Occultants are working on a project, codenamed Zeta, that would allow Acq Inc to get almost-always-accurate predictions of the future. Jim would like to recruit Alton, and the Tiffany Bleachers, to the elite teams of adventurers who further the completion of project Zeta through various tasks. He's willing to offer lots of types of support (although not financial beyond quest rewards) to each of the party members who need it, possibly specifically calling out Adelynn as her bounty hunters will be at the company party.
Maybe include Adelynn in the group that meets Jim?
If Alton accepts, he'll be given a secret flip-stone, which can only communicate things about Zeta. On the way home from the party, he'll receive the first text: a quest to complete a real dungeon in the jungles of Qajaar.
