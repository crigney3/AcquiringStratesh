# Planned Stuff
The party has entered [[Bask Pro Shops]] also known as Bask! This is just directly ancient egypt, big river delta in a desert and everything. There's also pyramids, and of course one of them is made of glass and run by the mercantile nobility who just happen to be fisherman and sell fishing supplies from the most professional pyramid in Bask. It's a Bask Pro Shop.
Of course, everyone here is mummies! Literally everyone. They're not usually hostile, but a lot of them have lost most of their sanity and just kinda shamble through their daily routines. The players can learn that this had something to do with The Great Desecrator, and learn that he was "a hawk-faced man who was advisor to the pharaoh."
One of the few passive NPCs is Tela’Maron, god of passage, who will let them into the ruins and potentially guide them. He is fully neutral to every occurrence, and prefers to spectate events. He will be the one to greet them and show them around Bask.
Some lore they might learn from him:
- Small gods have escaped before and not been caught, likely meaning that they died outside  Bask but possibly meaning there are Yomos pantheon gods from Bask
- It's unlikely the gods would stop trying to expand Bask's influence; the more people curious about Bask, the more chance that gods can get out
- The sun god Khaldun rules and takes most of the worship of the mummies - since they're not fully human anymore, there's not enough worship to go around for the gods
- Lesser gods Makara (death) and Ta-Menhit (war) help support Khaldun's rule
Other lore they can learn from exploring:
- Some of the mummies appear to once have been Verdan
- Possibly find some old writings that mention [[That-Which-Endures]] and its presence within Bask for some time, fled during the Desecration of Bask

So what to do?
- Killing Khaldun would collapse Bask, but that would probably be very hard
- Could instead find a way to wipe out the mummies; reducing faith in Khaldun would also collapse Bask
	- Of course, those are people, but they do kinda yearn for death
- Convince Khaldun and the other gods to leave Bask willingly, then smuggle them somewhere more normal where they can start again
