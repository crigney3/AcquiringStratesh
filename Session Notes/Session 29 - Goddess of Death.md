# Planned Stuff
The players have correctly picked out that Makara might be more susceptible to big changes in Bask, and they know she's interested in the whirlpool. When they visit her pyramid, they can persuade her for several things:
- Grant them Charon-style passage to the bottom of the whirlpool
- Plot to kill the mummies (she doesn't approve of everything being undead, it's not good for her business)
- Plot to kill Khaldun and Ta-Menhit
- Try to work with the party to persuade Khaldun and Ta-Menhit to take down Bask
- Offer to the party that breaking the crystal at the bottom of the whirlpool would release Bask entirely, placing it back in the world, but that noone's been able to do it
A lot of the rest of that encounter can be improv. Makara herself looks kind of like a drow wearing a regal outfit, but with spurs and ridges of bone sticking out across her body, often accented by her clothes.
The whirlpool itself contains the thing keeping Bask trapped - a 10ft cube (approx) of pure crystallized hatred, created by the Great Desecrator (they haven't heard the "hawk faced" description yet, try and include it) as an anchor for the dark magics he cast. Without them, the spells would fail and Bask would return. Where it would return is up to the person destroying the crystal, given checks.
Only mortals can harm the crystal, and it has many protections - namely summoning shadowy devils who can absorb divine magic to heal.
If they start heading to the crystal too quickly (it should be next session so I can make the battlemap) here's some things to slow them down:
- Khaldun destroying their Charon boat so they get separated in the whirlpool
- Being called to audience with Khaldun so he can ensure their worship
- finding the pile of junk that's fallen into Bask over the centuries (mostly rock and water but some other things)
- Finding the corpse of the guy who got sucked in due to Ironstand's mining operation, and starved to death in a locked room before Tela'Maron found him
