-   Session opens with “Ook” the orangutan knocking on the party’s “door” (which they still don’t have.) After a lengthy game of charades based on Ook’s drawing skills, the party figures out that he wants them to rescue his mother, who is caged in the private gardens of the Duchess Malentia Splatt. He offers a bag with about 50 gp, and an unidentified, but quite large, ruby.
    

-   During the quest, players will probably learn that Ook stole the ruby from Malentia. If they want to sell it, they’ll need to do so in a way that doesn’t link the sale to them. Possibly an in for Tori (The Grind probably has some connections to the criminal underworld) or Justin (if he can convince his father).
    

-   The party decides to gather more information, and lets Ook know that they’ll get back to him. He shows a place they can meet him, including a point where they can drop off his mother if they get her out. He also gives them a large purple flower, which he implies will stop her from attacking them since she is a poorly treated wild animal.
    
-   After Ook leaves, the party heads to Alixya’s place for a chili dinner which they kinda invited themselves to. Being gracious hosts, and being excited to meet Alyxia’s new friends, Kolvar and Mylaela serve dinner and ask about the “new business.”
