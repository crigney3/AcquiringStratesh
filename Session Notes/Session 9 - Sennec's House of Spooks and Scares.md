## Planned stuff
Session opens with each character going home for the night (or at least finding a way to get Alyxia home.) This is primarily so Alyxia can find the amulet in the attic, and so she can find the note from her parents saying that they're gone on an Acq Inc. Business Trip:tm: that could take weeks. Alyxia will know (read: be retconned a little into knowing) that this happens often, and that the huge list of things she has to do to keep the house in shape will be very annoying to juggle with her job. Still, she does live there.
The party follows a flyer they got to a "haunted house" outside of town. Given that ghosts are real and not always vengeful, the owner, Sennec Swiftheel, has hired actual ghosts and also added some illusion magic. He has had some living employees too, but he's down to one as they just keep "ghosting him" and quitting without notice (disappearing). That's because the one living employee, "Brad", has made a contract with a devil to consume people in the haunted house in exchange for power (and help covering up the disappearances.) This has ended up cursing the flyers advertising Sennec's House of Spooks and Scares to become cursed, and ask for help.
To complicate matters, The Tiffany Bleachers aren't the first adventuring party on the scene! The Formidable Five get there first, and Sennec says he'll only pay any adventuring party that succeeds and shows him proof there's something wrong, since he's not noticed anything and didn't call for any adventurers, much less two parties.
